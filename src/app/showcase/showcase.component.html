01 / Source

An experiment with many measurements

The source dataset describes a magnetic arch formed by two electron-cyclotron-resonance plasma sources. Its retarding potential analyzer (RPA) measurements vary the observation angle across five magnetic configurations; the DA configuration also includes Faraday Cup (FC) data.

The files stay with the source repository. This example records their names and SHA-256 fingerprints, not the measurement bytes.
