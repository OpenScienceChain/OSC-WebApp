Why provenance matters

Evidence that travels with the work

OSC records provenance events through Hyperledger Fabric, a permissioned blockchain. Metadata, file fingerprints, authorship, organization, and revisions stay connected in an append-only history that collaborators can inspect later.

What the blockchain adds

Trust infrastructure that stays out of the way

Researchers use familiar forms and catalogs. OSC handles the ledger transaction behind the scenes and returns evidence that can be checked by collaborators, organizations, and future maintainers.

  • No wallet or cryptocurrency. Approved organizations manage identities.
  • Research files stay off-chain. OSC records their fingerprints and context.
  • Updates do not erase history. Each accepted revision becomes a new event.
